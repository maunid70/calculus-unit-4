Labs: watch the rates move

Seven interactive sheets. Press play, drag across the graph to scrub time, and check the answer table against your homework.

Shrinking cylinder

Height fixed at 7 in, radius shrinking 3 in/s. Find dV/dt.

The rate is −42πr in³/s, so halving the radius halves the rate.

Open sheet 1

Two cars and an angle

Yellow car east at 60 mph, white car south toward the corner at 40 mph. Find dθ/dt at x = 8, y = 6.

Uses tan θ = y/x. Watch θ fall from π/2 to 0.

Open sheet 2

Growing soap bubble

Air goes in at 8π cm³/s. When V = 288π cm³, find r, dr/dt and dS/dt.

At that instant r = 6 cm, and the radius grows more slowly as the bubble gets bigger.

Open sheet 3

Draining water tank

Radius 4 ft, height falling 0.1 ft/s. How fast is the volume changing?

This is the Day 4 check-your-understanding problem: −1.6π ft³/s, the same at every moment.

Open sheet 4

Velocity Raptor

Motion on a line: pick a case from the sign tables and launch the raptor.

Same signs on v and a means speeding up; opposite signs means slowing down.

Open sheet 5

Evaporating cone

Cone 14 m tall, 8 m across, depth falling 0.1 m/day. Find dr/dt.

−1/35 m/day, and since dV/dt = −(2π/245)h², the volume falls faster when h is large.

Open sheet 6

Water in, water out

Water flows in at E(t) and leaks out at L(t), crossing at t = 7.

Rising while E > L, falling while L > E, and not changing at t = 7.
