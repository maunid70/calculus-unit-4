    FMethod

    Step 1

    Write the model.

    V = πr²h

    Volume ties radius and height together. Every rate on this sheet comes from it.

    Step 2

    Fix the constant.

    V = 7πr²

    h never changes, so it goes in before differentiating. r can’t: it is still moving.

    Step 3

    Differentiate in t.

    dV/dt = 14πr · dr/dt

    The chain rule brings out dr/dt. 14πr is the side-wall area, 2πrh.

    Step 4

    Substitute last.

    −42π(12) ≈ −1583 in³/s

    Use dr/dt = −3 and the radius at the moment you care about.
